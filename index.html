Who I Am

Crafting Reality Through Effects

I'm ABXZ — a self-taught visual effects artist and motion designer who lives at the intersection of code, color, and creativity. Born from the raw aesthetic of underground editing culture, my work blends hyper-stylized motion, cinematic grading, and digital art into a signature visual language.

Every frame is a canvas. Every cut is intentional. Whether it's a reel, a brand film, or a social edit — I bring obsessive attention to detail and a relentless drive for originality.

Available for collaborations, brand projects, short films, social content, and creative consulting.

The Story

Creative Journey

2020

The Beginning

Started experimenting with video edits on a basic laptop — no professional gear, just curiosity and hours of YouTube tutorials. First edits uploaded on Instagram.

2021

First Viral Edit

A transition reel hit 100K views organically. The Instagram community noticed. DMs started flooding in. The journey got serious.

2022

Going Professional

First paid clients, first brand deals. Invested in proper software — After Effects, DaVinci Resolve. Developed a signature cinematic color language.

2023

Scaling Up

Collaborated with 50+ creators and brands. Expanded into VFX compositing and motion graphics. Built a growing network across India's creator economy.

2024–Present

Full-Stack Visual Artist

Now operating as a full creative studio — VFX, color, 3D, motion, brand identity. Open for international projects and creative partnerships.
